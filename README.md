🎬 BingeLens-AI
</br>
Stop scrolling. Start feeling.

BingeLens-AI is a smart recommendation engine designed to solve "analysis paralysis." Instead of browsing endless genres, BingeLens uses AI to analyze your current emotional state—whether you're "productively procrastinating," "need a cathartic cry," or "craving a 90s nostalgia trip"—and maps it to the perfect cinematic match.

🚀 Key Features:
</br>
1) Mood-to-Metadata Mapping: Goes beyond basic genres to find movies based on emotional resonance and "vibe."
2) Natural Language Input: Tell the AI exactly how your day went, and let it do the heavy lifting.
3) Dynamic Discovery: Powered by OpenAI to understand nuanced human sentiment.
4) The "Vibe" Filter: Adjust intensity levels from "Background Noise" to "Total Immersion."
